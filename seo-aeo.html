SEARCH + SOCIAL

Connect search demand to the social content your audience responds to.

AI.IQ includes SEO and answer-oriented content tools, but the aim is not to chase ranking hacks. The useful combination is your first-party performance evidence plus real search-demand data.

Keyword Planner intelligence

Import Google Keyword Planner exports to compare average monthly searches, competition, trend changes and bid ranges. Use the findings to prioritise themes, questions and content angles that are worth testing.

Search Console intelligence

Search Console exports can show which queries and pages already receive impressions and clicks. That can reveal questions, topics or wording that deserve stronger supporting social content.

AI Overviews and generative search

For Google Search, AI visibility still depends on normal Search eligibility: useful public content, crawlable pages, clear technical structure and indexability. AI.IQ does not promise placement in AI Overviews; it helps organise evidence and content ideas that can support a people-first search strategy.
